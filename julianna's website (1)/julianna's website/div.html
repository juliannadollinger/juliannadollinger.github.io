All About Jupiter

     Jupiter is the fifth farthest planet from the sun, and the largest of any planets in the solar system. It is a gas giant mainly made out of hydrogen and helium, and it has a diameter of about 11 Earths. It has many, many moons each with their own exciting characteristics. A distinct things that most people notice about Jupiter are its many storms. The most notable of which stands out amoung the others, and has a very creative name of 'The Great Red Spot'. It can be seen in the background image of this page.

The Weirdest Things About Jupiter

-Even though it is the largest planet, it spins rediculously fast. 
 A day on Jupiter (called a Jovian day) is only 10 hours.

-Jupiter has a very low density of 1.326g/cm. Compare 
this to Earth's density, which is 5.5 g/cm3

-It is a crazily massive planet, and if you added 
the mass of all the other planets together 
and then multiplied them by 2.5 it would 
be equal to the mass of Jupiter
