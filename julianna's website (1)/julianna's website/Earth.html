Earth Fast Facts

Earth is the only planet we have discovered that is known to have life. It has a thick atomosphere, and paired with an abundance of water, it is able to support life as we know it. Millions of years of evolution have come to create every living thing on/in our infinitely conplex planet. Billions of years of time and space have lead up to your exsistence, and it will then continue without you. Earth has lots of water on it, with around 70% of its surface covered in water. This planet is the third farthest from the sun, and because it is in what is considered the 'Goldilocks Zone', H2O, AKA water, is able to exsist in solid, liquid, and gas form. This property of a planet is thought to be an important factor that contributed to the formation of life. Based on where you are on Earth, geography and climate can be extremely different, with a perfectly adapted ecosystem in each location.
