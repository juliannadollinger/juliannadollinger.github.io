All About Venus

Venus is the second fathest planet from the sun, and also the hottest. This may at first confuse people, but a closer look at Venus' atomospheric structure explains this perfectly. Venus has an extremely thick atomosphere with a large amount of CO2, which creates the green-house effect. This is when the energy from the suns rays enters the atomosphere, but is then trapped by the thick atomoshpere. This causes Venus to have a daytime temperature of around 854F! Despite this difference, Venus is considered Earth's "sister planet" because they are similiar in mass and size.

-A day on Venus is longer than a year on Venus. If we have interplanetary traveling in the future, anyone wanting to spend just a day on Venus would actually need to stay more than a year! This is because of Venus' orbit and rotation, which makes a single spin for the planet last a bit longer than the period of revolution.
